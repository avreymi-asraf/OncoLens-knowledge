# The Asta ScholarQA and Asta Paper Finder baselines are variants of the production systems behind Asta Summarize Literature and Asta Find Papers respectively.

ScholarQA is a long-form QA system with retrieval and reranking built for the SQA task; Paper Finder targets the PaperFinder task.
